# Sophie's GCSE Revision

A calm revision app with one card per question and no timers. It is one web page (`index.html`), so it runs in any modern browser with nothing to install.

**Open it:** https://sirlegend123.github.io/revision/

## What it does

- **Today's mission.** Up to 8 cards a day. Each card sits in one of five memory boxes and comes back after 1, 2, 4, 7 and then 14 days. A card that isn't known yet goes back to box 1.
- **Four games per topic.** Quick quiz, Flip cards, Match the words and Order the steps.
- **Help on every card.** Hints, ratio tables or worked steps, memory hooks, and read aloud.
- **Built-in topic.** Ratio and Proportion.
- **New topics.** Copy the app's instructions into Claude with your notes or photos, then paste the reply back. You can also type cards in yourself.
- **Make it comfortable.** Background colour, light or dark, text size, wider letter spacing, and sounds on or off.
- **Saving.** Progress is saved in the browser on this device. Backup files move it to another device.

## Publishing it (GitHub Pages)

1. In this repository on GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`.
3. Save. After a minute or two the app is live at the link above.

The maths games are at https://sirlegend123.github.io/Educational-games/.
